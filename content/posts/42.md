Summary: How to finish a project?

To escape the cycle of unfinished work, the author developed the following strategies:

1. **Define "Done" from the Start**: Set clear criteria for what constitutes a finished project to prevent scope creep.
2. **Embrace MVP**: Focus on releasing a minimal viable product (MVP) rather than aiming for perfection.
3. **Time-Box Projects**: Set a deadline to create urgency and avoid endless iterations.
4. **Practice Finishing Small Things**: Build the habit of finishing by completing smaller projects or tasks regularly.
5. **Separate Ideation from Implementation**: Log new ideas for future projects instead of acting on them immediately.
6. **Celebrate Completions**: Reward yourself when you finish a project, reinforcing the habit of completion.
7. **Embrace Accountability**: Find an accountability partner or make public commitments to stay motivated.
